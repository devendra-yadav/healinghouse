Cash Flow

Real money in vs. real money out — appointment cash payments, package sales, and wallet top-ups as inflow; expenses, wallet refunds, and package refunds as outflow. Unlike the other reports, this is cash-basis, not accrual: money already counted here (a package sale, a wallet top-up) is never counted again when it's later drawn down against an appointment.
